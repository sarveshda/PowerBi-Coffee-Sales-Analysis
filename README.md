# PowerBi-Coffee-Sales-Analysis

☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard
I created an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze Coffee Shop Sales for May 2023. This report helps understand sales trends, customer demand, product performance, and store-wise contribution using dynamic visuals and filters.

📌 Dashboard Overview
The Power BI report includes:
Monthly slicer and calendar view for date selection
Dynamic visuals that update based on selected dates
Sales comparison with Last Month (LM)
Breakdown by product category, product type, store location, and weekdays vs weekends

📈 Sales Performance Analysis
Total Sales: $156.7K
Total Orders: 33.5K
Total Quantity Sold: 48K units
 All metrics show strong growth compared to last month, indicating increased customer demand and higher transaction volume.

📅 Date-Level & Drill-Down Analysis
Daily sales trend shows consistent performance across the month
Selecting a specific date (e.g., 10 May 2023) displays:
Total sales, orders, and quantity for that day
A visual breakdown to analyze daily contribution
This feature allows drill-down analysis to understand daily sales behavior.

📆 Weekday vs Weekend Sales
Weekdays: 74% of total revenue
Weekends: 26% of total revenue
The data highlights higher weekday sales, suggesting business is driven mainly by working-day customers.

🏬 Store Location Analysis
Hell’s Kitchen, Astoria, and Lower Manhattan show similar sales contribution
Each location demonstrates 30%+ growth vs last month
This indicates consistent performance across all stores.

☕ Product Category & Product Type Analysis
Coffee is the highest revenue-generating category
Tea shows faster growth compared to other categories
Top-performing products include:
Barista Espresso
Brewed Chai Tea
Hot Chocolate
This section helps identify best-selling and fast-growing products.

🔍 Key Insights from Power BI Report
Strong month-over-month sales growth
Stable daily sales trend
Higher revenue on weekdays
Growing demand for tea and specialty beverages
Balanced performance across store locations
